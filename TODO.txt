Measured and not done, so it stays out unless the numbers change:

- Decoder header scratch to avoid copying a read that completes a split
  header: no measurable gain even when every read splits a header; the copy
  is a cache-hot 4 KiB memmove once per hundred reads on real traffic.
- Encoding straight into destination storage: only the client's masking
  path copies, and clients are not the hot side of this library.
- A message-scoped io.Writer: Read, WriteTo and WriteFrom cover the shapes;
  ergonomics only.
- Automatic switching between attached and shared compression: the
  crossover depends on message size and connection count; README states it.
- A reactor or event-loop API, and the epoll/io_uring write pump: see
  ws/DESIGN.md, Later.
